PROJECT: Life OS

Life OS is a personal system for tracking habits, daily check-ins (mood, energy,
sleep), and life decisions, then turning that data into real self-insight.
Instead of generic advice, it studies your own patterns - for example noticing
that your habit success drops on low-sleep days - and eventually predicts risks
(like a streak about to break) and recommends adjustments based on what has
actually worked for you before. It is built as a full product: an Express.js +
MySQL backend and a separate frontend, connected over a REST API, developed in
phases with production-realistic practices (Docker, proper data modeling,
testing) rather than as a throwaway script. This is currently a learning
project and portfolio piece, with the long-term goal of possibly becoming a
real product people can use.
